## **Magnetic Tape** 
new we getting to the era where we actually see the things in real aka magnetic tape. 
![cdf7041392ddfc346fec25502da908a0.png](../_resources/cdf7041392ddfc346fec25502da908a0.png)

The "Tape" itself is a long ribbon of plastic. One side is coated with a thin layer of magnetic material. This material is made of billions of tiny magnetic particles that act like microscopic compass needles. When the tape is "blank," these particles are oriented randomly.
